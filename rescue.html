Ledger · Rescue

Recover your data.

This page reads your stored Ledger data directly. Download a backup before doing anything else.

Step 1 · Inspect what's stored

Check that your data is still there.

Step 2 · Download backup

Save a copy to your phone before any other action. This is critical.

Step 3 · Repair (try this first)

Attempts to clean up corrupt entries (null items, broken arrays) while keeping all valid data. Safe to try.

Step 4 · Hard reset (last resort)

Wipes Ledger data from this device. Only do this AFTER downloading the backup above. You can re-import the backup into the main app later.

Step 5 · Return to app

After repair or reset, go back to the main app.
